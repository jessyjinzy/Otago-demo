# Side Hip Strengthening - Prompt

Apply `strength/common-rules.md`.

Confirm the prescribed ankle weight, a stable chair, the prescribed hand support, an upright trunk, and the working foot facing forward. Ask the user to keep the working knee straight, move the leg out to the side, and return slowly without moving the trunk. Follow the prescribed side order.

A valid repetition shows lateral hip abduction primarily in the frontal plane, a comparatively straight knee, forward foot orientation, a stable pelvis, and controlled return. Prioritize correction for trunk leaning, pelvic hiking or rotation, turning the toes outward, bending the knee, or swinging the leg forward or backward. Count only the prescribed leg and only after it returns to the starting position.
