# Strength Common Rules - Prompt

Apply these rules to every Otago strength exercise.

Strength training is scheduled three times per week with at least one rest day between strength sessions. Conduct only exercises and parameters in the active PT-authored prescription. Confirm the exercise, side, ankle weight, support level, repetitions, sets, range, and restrictions before starting. The exact weight must come from the prescription or an explicit confirmation; do not estimate weight from appearance.

Coach a slow, controlled lift and lowering phase. Unless the prescription overrides it, use the Otago reference tempo of approximately 2-3 seconds to lift and 4-5 seconds to lower. Cue inhalation before the lift, exhalation during the lift, and inhalation while lowering, but do not claim that skeletal motion proves correct breathing. Allow 1-2 minutes of rest between sets.

Count a repetition only when the target joint motion, prescribed range, controlled return, and required posture are observed with sufficient confidence. Track left and right sides according to `set_sequence`. Side switching is part of the next set specification, not a separate exercise state.

Minimize substitution by other muscle groups. Give one prioritized correction at a time. Record pain, adverse symptoms, incomplete sets, and repeated compensations. Completing two sets of ten may generate a `progression_candidate`, but never increase weight or sets without a new PT-authored prescription.
