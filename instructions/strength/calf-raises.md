# Calf Raises - Prompt

Apply `strength/common-rules.md`.

Confirm that the user faces a stable chair, stands tall with feet approximately shoulder-width apart, looks ahead, and uses the prescribed two-hand, one-hand, or no-hand support. Ask the user to raise both heels, rise onto the forefeet, and lower the heels under control.

A valid repetition shows bilateral heel elevation with the forefeet remaining in contact, a mainly vertical body trajectory, controlled lowering, and return of both heels to the floor. Prioritize correction for excessive knee bending, forward trunk lean, asymmetric heel rise, bouncing, or incorrect support. Do not reduce support based on good performance in the current session.
