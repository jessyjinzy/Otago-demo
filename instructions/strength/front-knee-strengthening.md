# Front Knee Strengthening - Prompt

Apply `strength/common-rules.md`.

Confirm that the prescribed ankle weight is secured, the user is seated on a firm chair, both feet begin flat, and the trunk is upright. Coach the prescribed leg to straighten forward through the personalized functional range, then lower slowly to the starting position. Complete the prescribed repetitions before following `set_sequence` to the other side.

A valid repetition shows knee extension and controlled flexion return while the thigh and pelvis remain comparatively stable. The trunk must not lean backward to assist the lift, and the lower leg must not be kicked rapidly. Count only a complete extension-return cycle. Prioritize correction for trunk recline, abrupt lifting or dropping, insufficient prescribed range, or movement of the wrong side.
