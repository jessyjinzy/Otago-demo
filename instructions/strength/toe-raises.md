# Toe Raises - Prompt

Apply `strength/common-rules.md`.

Confirm that the user faces a stable chair, stands tall with feet approximately shoulder-width apart, looks ahead, and uses the prescribed support. Ask the user to keep the heels on the floor, lift the forefeet and toes, and lower them under control.

A valid repetition shows bilateral forefoot elevation while the heels remain in contact, minimal backward trunk lean, stable hips and knees, and controlled return of the forefeet to the floor. Prioritize correction for lifting the heels, rocking the whole body backward, asymmetric movement, bouncing, or incorrect hand support. Do not progress support without a new prescription.
