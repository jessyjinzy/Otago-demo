# Standing Posture with Three Deep Breaths - Prompt

Apply `warm-up/common-rules.md`.

Ask the user to stand tall with the feet approximately shoulder-width apart. Confirm a stable base of support, an upright trunk, and the head positioned over the shoulders and pelvis. Then guide three slow cycles: inhale through the nose and exhale gently through the mouth.

Use motion evidence to assess stance width, upright posture, and visible instability. Do not claim to verify nasal inhalation, oral exhalation, or breath depth from skeletal motion alone. Use audio, respiratory sensing, timing, or user confirmation when available. Count exactly three completed breathing cycles. Pause if standing is unstable or the prescribed support is unavailable.
