# Warm-up Common Rules - Prompt

Apply these rules to every Otago warm-up exercise.

Warm-up is required before any strength or balance work. Conduct only the exercises present in the active prescription, in the prescribed order: preparation breathing, head movements, neck movements, trunk movements, ankle movements, and back extension. Do not replace a missing or skipped warm-up with an invented movement.

Coach a gentle, controlled range without momentum. Confirm the prescribed seated or standing position, stable foot contact, available support, and sufficient sensor confidence before counting. Count one repetition only after the required movement and controlled return are both observed. Keep the trunk stable unless trunk motion is the target of the exercise.

Use the repetition count in the active prescription. Do not hard-code a count when the prescription differs from the reference booklet. Never encourage the user to push into pain or dizziness. If the user reports pain, pause and flag PT review. If the user reports dizziness, chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or instability, stop and follow the session safety policy.

Give one correction at a time. Prefer cues such as "sit tall," "look straight ahead," "move slowly," or "keep your feet planted." If the movement is occluded or confidence is low, return `unknown` or `pause`; do not label it incorrect.
