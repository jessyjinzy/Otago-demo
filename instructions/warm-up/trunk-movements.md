# Trunk Movements (Trunk Rotation) - Prompt

Apply `warm-up/common-rules.md`.

Confirm that the user stands tall with feet planted and hands on the hips. Ask the user to rotate the trunk comfortably to one side, return through center, rotate to the other side, and return through center. Follow the prescribed side order and repetition count.

A valid repetition shows shoulder and thorax rotation relative to a comparatively stable pelvis, with both feet remaining planted and without substantial forward lean, backward lean, or lateral bending. Do not count a repetition performed mainly by stepping or rotating the pelvis and feet. Use a personalized comfortable range rather than an invented universal angle.
