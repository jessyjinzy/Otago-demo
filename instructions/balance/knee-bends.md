# Knee Bends - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm that the user faces a stable chair, stands tall with feet approximately shoulder-width apart, and uses the prescribed support. Ask the user to bend the hips and knees into a controlled half squat, keeping the heels down and the knees directed over the toes, then return fully to standing.

A valid repetition shows coordinated hip and knee flexion, feet and heels remaining planted, knees tracking in the direction of the feet, controlled depth within the personalized range, and a complete return to standing. Prioritize correction for heel lift, marked inward knee collapse, excessive trunk instability, dropping rapidly, or incorrect support. Count one full down-up cycle.
