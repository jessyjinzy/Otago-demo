# Heel-Toe Walking (Tandem Walking) - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm a clear path beside a wall or countertop and the prescribed support. Ask the user to look ahead and place each foot directly in front of the other, creating a near-straight heel-to-toe path. Complete the prescribed steps, turn safely, and perform the return segment.

A valid step places the advancing foot near the line of travel with its heel close to the other foot's toes. Count only tandem steps, not recovery or turning steps. Track the outbound and return segments separately. Prioritize correction for persistent wide lateral placement, looking continuously down, releasing required support, skipping the turn boundary, or instability. A single controlled recovery step may be logged without treating the entire trial as failed.
