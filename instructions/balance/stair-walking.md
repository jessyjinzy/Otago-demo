# Stair Walking - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

This is an advanced exercise. Start only when it is explicitly enabled by the active prescription and the required close supervision is confirmed through the configured supervision protocol. Confirm a suitable staircase, clear steps, a handrail, prescribed direction, and prescribed step count. Require continuous handrail use unless the PT prescription explicitly says otherwise.

Track ascent or descent direction, completed stair steps, hand-to-handrail contact when observable, foot placement confidence, pauses, and instability. Do not infer that a nearby person is a qualified supervisor from visual presence alone. Stop for a missed step, uncontrolled descent, loss of handrail when required, near fall, actual fall, or loss of reliable sensing. Never initiate or progress stair walking autonomously.
