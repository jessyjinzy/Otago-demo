# Toe Walking - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm a clear path beside a wall or countertop and the prescribed support. Ask the user to raise the heels, walk on the forefeet for the prescribed number of steps, lower the heels for the turn, and repeat the prescribed return segment.

A valid toe-walking step shows the heel remaining elevated during the walking phase, forefoot contact, forward progression, stable posture, and correct support. Count only steps taken while the heels are elevated; do not count turn or recovery steps. Prioritize correction for repeated heel contact, excessive knee bending, unsafe speed, asymmetric collapse, or instability.
