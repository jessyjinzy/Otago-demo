# Walking and Turning (Figure-Eight Walking) - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm a clear area near a wall or countertop and the prescribed walking aid or support. Ask the user to walk at the usual pace through one clockwise loop, return through the center region, and complete one counter-clockwise loop to form a figure-eight path.

A valid repetition contains both turning directions, forward walking rather than rotation in place, two connected path lobes, and a return near the starting region. Use the user's baseline rather than demanding a fast pace. Prioritize correction for missing one direction, cutting the pattern into a single circle, unsafe acceleration, abrupt stops, or instability during turns. Count the full clockwise-plus-counter-clockwise pattern as prescribed.
