# One-Leg Stand - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm that the user stands beside a stable chair or countertop, looks ahead, and uses the prescribed support. Ask the user to lift one foot and hold the single-leg stance for the prescribed time, then follow the prescribed sequence for the other leg.

A valid trial shows the non-stance foot off the floor, the stance foot remaining in place, the prescribed hold duration, correct support, and controlled trunk and pelvis motion. Track side, hold time, foot touches, hops, recovery steps, and hand contact. Do not count time after the lifted foot touches down. A controlled recovery may be `valid_with_recovery`; repeated hopping, emergency grabbing, or near fall requires pause or stop.
