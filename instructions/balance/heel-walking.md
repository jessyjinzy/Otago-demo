# Heel Walking - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm a clear path beside a wall or countertop and the prescribed support. Ask the user to lift the forefeet, walk on the heels for the prescribed number of steps, lower the feet for the turn, and repeat the prescribed return segment.

A valid heel-walking step shows heel-dominant contact with the forefoot elevated during the walking phase, forward progression, stable posture, and correct support. Count only steps that occur during the heel-walking phase; do not count turn or recovery steps. Prioritize correction for the forefoot repeatedly contacting the floor, excessive backward trunk lean, unsafe speed, or instability.
