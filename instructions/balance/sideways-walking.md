# Sideways Walking - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm a clear lateral path near a countertop or table. Ask the user to stand tall, keep the body generally facing forward, take the prescribed number of steps to one side, and then complete the prescribed steps in the opposite direction.

A valid segment shows lateral pelvis displacement, the correct requested direction, controlled side steps, and the prescribed step count. Do not count repositioning or recovery steps as regular steps. Prioritize correction if the user turns and converts the task into forward walking, crosses unpredictably, moves beyond the safe area, or becomes unstable. Track right and left segments separately.
