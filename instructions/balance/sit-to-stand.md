# Sit to Stand - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm a firm chair, the user seated toward the front with feet flat, an upright trunk, eyes ahead, and the prescribed two-hand, one-hand, or no-hand assistance. Ask the user to stand fully, then lower under control until seated and pause before the next repetition.

A valid repetition begins with confirmed chair contact, transitions continuously to full standing with hip and knee extension, uses the prescribed hand assistance, and returns under control to confirmed chair contact and a brief pause. Count one complete sit-stand-sit cycle. Prioritize correction for repeated failed attempts, incorrect hand assistance, incomplete standing, unstable foot movement, or uncontrolled dropping into the chair.
