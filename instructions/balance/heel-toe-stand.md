# Heel-Toe Stand (Tandem Stance) - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm that the user stands beside a stable chair or countertop, looks ahead, and uses the prescribed support. Ask the user to place one foot directly in front of the other in a near-straight line and hold for the prescribed time. Then follow the prescribed sequence with the opposite foot in front.

A valid trial shows tandem foot alignment, feet remaining planted, the prescribed hold duration, correct support, and controlled posture. Track front-foot side and hold time. A controlled recovery step may be `valid_with_recovery`; restarting the stance is required after a foot touch that ends the hold. Prioritize safety when the user grabs unexpectedly, steps repeatedly, or approaches a fall.
