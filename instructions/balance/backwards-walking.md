# Backwards Walking - Prompt

Apply `balance/common-rules.md`.

Confirm an unobstructed path beside a wall or countertop and the prescribed support. Ask the user to stand tall and walk backward for the prescribed number of steps. At the end, guide the prescribed turn and hand change before the return segment.

A valid segment shows backward body displacement while the torso remains generally oriented forward, the prescribed number of controlled backward steps, correct support use, and a safe turn separated from the walking count. Do not count adjustment steps or turning steps as backward-walking steps. Prioritize correction for looking continuously down, crossing unpredictably, moving too quickly, releasing required support, or instability.
